A polycule of four girlfriends.
Elk, Kitty, P3, P4.
They've finished their adventure and are on their way back.
They're exhausted, and burned out.
They're resting at a cliffside, with a campfire.
As they talk, they reflect on adventures they just had.

We never know what the main story was.

Select a character. They tell their part of the tale of exploring, collecting, struggling. During the tale you (re)collect memory fragments.

When back at the camp, you can use your completed memory to have a one-to-one with that partner when reaching a threshold.

The next tale begins. As they recount their stories around the campfire, they hold each other up, boost each others' morale, and bolster their resolve to make it back home.

Level 1:
Choose between Elk/P3, then do the other.
They have a one-to-one.
Then Kitty/P4, then the other.
They can choose any of the 4 to put points into.

Elk -> P3
P3 -> Elk
Kitty -> P3/Elk/P4
P4 -> P3/Elk/P4

Level 2
Intro at the cliffside.
Then, choose from any 4. Put into each other for 4 one-to-ones with any they have not yet had one with (Elk -> P3, P3 -> Elk will be definitely taken)

Elk -> Kitty/P4
P3 -> Kitty/P4
Kitty -> P3/Elk/P4 (minus one that's done)
P4 -> P3/Elk/Kitty (minus one that's done)

Level 3:
Intro at the cliffside.
Same again. Each character has their third one-to-ones.

Level 4:
Intro at the cliffside.
Everyone's final one-to-ones.

Ending:
Morale and romance depends on completion % - 100% has everyone full of life.
